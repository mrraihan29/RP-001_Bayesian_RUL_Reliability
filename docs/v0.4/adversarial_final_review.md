## Checks that survived adversarial review

The analysis summary records 33 completed MCMC fits, 32 passing configured diagnostics and one failure, `v04_syn_high_rho_3`. It separately preserves four failed canonical preparation attempts. Those are not four extra MCMC fits. The precision summary reports a PASS with max approximate upper MCSE 0.220513 cycles, all 225 independent-fit comparisons and all nine oracle comparisons compatible, and the separate known-target estimator validation passed. This supports the prescribed training-development numerical gate for the cases tested; it does not certify future endpoint precision.

All eight pipeline perturbations completed, all 24 role stages met their minimum eligible counts, and the raw artifact says no official or protected data were accessed and no anchor outcomes were attached. Selection varied across the four paired cutoff comparisons; the CQR correction ranges from 0 to 62.981 cycles and Bayesian post-hoc q from 0 to 13.166. Report06 correctly treats these as fixed-anchor sensitivity summaries, not assessment scores, independent performance comparisons, or a total-pipeline variance estimate.

The structural-identification argument in report08 is explicitly a population-moment result under its stated rank and covariance assumptions, and separates that result from finite-sample estimation, practical precision, physical interpretation, and fitted-model checks. The prior report describes a working elicitation after historical calibration exposure, not an empirically validated reliability prior; actual posterior-contraction records now use matching prior references, and fixed rho is marked as fixed rather than data contraction. The cutoff-survivor mechanism, conformal transport, development calibration exposure, CQR/Bayesian feature differences, unequal search budgets, and absence of a practical margin are all disclosed without claiming a winner.

The reports and artifacts continue to prohibit official-test access and label scoring. No test predictions, official coverage, confirmatory claim, or real-engine reliability conclusion appears in the reviewed material. The prior and PPC summaries are bounded diagnostics; neither establishes fitted-model adequacy or transport.

The available registry CPU fields sum to about 2,467 seconds, the longest recorded run is about 113 seconds, and the v0.4 experiment tree is about 134 MB. Those observed values are below the six CPU-hour, ten-minute-per-fit, and 2 GiB limits. The final compute ledger should still reconcile any repair-process time not represented in registry CPU fields and confirm the two-campaign allowance explicitly.
